Welcome to Mermaid Tales from Hollygrove Cove

Mermaid Tales from Hollygrove Cove is a collection of stories, photographs, memories, and magical moments inspired by the merfolk, performers, volunteers, vendors, and guests who make Hollygrove such a special place.

Created by a former mermaid of the Cove, this site exists to preserve the friendships, adventures, traditions, laughter, and wonder experienced within Hollygrove Cove and throughout the Michigan Renaissance Festival.

Whether you are revisiting old memories, discovering the Cove for the first time, or simply looking for a little magic in your day, we invite you to explore the stories, meet the merfolk, and become part of the adventure.

About the Cove

Hollygrove Cove is home to the merfolk of the Michigan Renaissance Festival, a gathering of talented performers from many places who come together each season to create unforgettable experiences for festival guests.

Through storytelling, photographs, and cherished memories, this site celebrates the people and traditions that have made Hollygrove Cove a beloved destination for visitors and cast members alike.
