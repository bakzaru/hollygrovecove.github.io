Welcome to the Cove

Welcome to Mermaid Tales from Hollygrove Cove, a place where memories, stories, photographs, and a little bit of magic come together.

Created by Laura, a former mermaid of the Cove, this site celebrates the performers, volunteers, artisans, guests, and unforgettable moments that make Hollygrove special.

Whether you're revisiting old memories, discovering Hollygrove for the first time, or simply enjoying a good story, we invite you to wander the paths, meet the characters, and share in the magic.
