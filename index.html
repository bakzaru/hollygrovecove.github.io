Welcome to the Cove

Welcome to Mermaid Tales from Hollygrove Cove, a collection of stories, photographs, memories, and magical moments inspired by the people who make Hollygrove such a special place.

Through personal stories, festival memories, and glimpses behind the scenes, this site hopes to preserve the wonder that keeps visitors returning year after year.

Whether you are revisiting old memories, discovering Hollygrove for the first time, or simply enjoying a good story, we invite you to wander the paths, meet the characters, and share in the magic.

About Laura

For years, Laura helped bring a little extra magic to Hollygrove as one of the Cove's merfolk. Through stories, photographs, and memories, she hopes to preserve the wonder that makes Hollygrove such a special place for performers, volunteers, vendors, and guests.

Mermaid Tales from Hollygrove Cove is her way of sharing those memories while celebrating the people who keep the spirit of Hollygrove alive.
